    About sznd.

    sznd. is read as seasoned. It's for people who have been through things, been weathered, and moved through more than one season of life.

    We make quality clothing with real thought and design behind it. Clean basics and a few art-led pieces, each with a story behind it. Nothing loud, nothing flashy.

    It began in Bengaluru as a creative outlet and a quiet act of faith. Every piece is made to order, so nothing is made that isn't wanted.

    Ron, founder
